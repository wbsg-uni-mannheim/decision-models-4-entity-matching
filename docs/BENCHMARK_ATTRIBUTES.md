# Attributes per benchmark

Attributes of the records that the decision models and the generative LLMs receive, per benchmark (Section "Experimental Setup" of the paper). The attribute names in the serialized records are those of the benchmark files.

| Benchmark | Attributes used |
|---|---|
| Abt-Buy | name, description, price |
| Walmart-Amazon | title, category, brand, model number, price |
| WDC Products | brand, title, description, price, price currency |
| DBLP-ACM | title, authors, venue, year |
| DBLP-Scholar | title, authors, venue, year |
| Dn7 Walmart-Amazon | title, model number, price, shipping weight, brand, dimensions |
| billiger.de 100un | brand, name, description, price |
| Semi-HETER | title, authors, publisher, year, ISBN, pages, price, extra |
